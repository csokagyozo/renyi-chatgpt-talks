# Chat vs. Work in ChatGPT

**Recommendation:** Use **Chat** as your default for mathematical exploration. Use **Work** when you can specify a substantial research task and the deliverable you want.

## The difference

**Chat** is a conversational reasoning environment. It is well suited to questioning assumptions, developing proofs, testing conjectures, and finding counterexamples. For demanding problems, select a strong reasoning model such as **Pro**, if available.

**Work** is an agentic execution environment. It can coordinate multi-step tasks across available tools, files, and information sources, then produce materials for your review. You can monitor progress, intervene, and redirect it.

| | Chat | Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Optimizes for** | Iterative reasoning and exploration | Autonomous task execution |
| **Researcher's role** | Guide and scrutinize each step | Specify goal, constraints, and review criteria |
| **Best for** | Open-ended problems and evolving conjectures | Literature surveys and structured research workflows |
| **Typical result** | Proof ideas, objections, counterexamples, sharper questions | Comparison tables, bibliographies, reports, organized outputs |

## Two mathematical use cases

**Chat: investigate a conjecture** 
*“Might this spectral-gap inequality hold for a broader class of operators? Identify essential assumptions, seek counterexamples, and explore proof strategies.”*

The question evolves as the researcher evaluates each argument. Even a counterexample can be a valuable result.

**Work: map a research area** 
*“Survey published spectral-gap estimates for this operator class. Compare assumptions and conclusions, verify the source papers, and prepare an annotated bibliography.”*

The objective is specified, but accomplishing it involves multiple sources and steps.

## Why start with Chat?

**Theoretical research is usually exploratory, not deliverable-driven.** Definitions, assumptions, and even the central question often change during investigation. The researcher must repeatedly assess intermediate claims and decide which direction is promising. Chat keeps this critical judgment inside a tight feedback loop.

**Work becomes especially useful when an open-ended investigation produces a bounded subtask**, for example, collecting literature, comparing known results, or preparing a structured research summary.

> **Rule of thumb:** Still figuring out **what the right question is**? Choose **Chat**. Know **what result to produce and how to check it**? Consider **Work**.
